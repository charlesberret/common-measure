# Access (draft)

**Status:** goals and leans — **not** terms of sale, **not** legal advice  
Promote to customer-facing terms only after review. See also [attribution.md](attribution.md).

Common Measure’s access design has three seats on purpose:

1. **Anyone** can use free aggregates.
2. **Academics and libraries** are first-class, not a coupon code added later.
3. **Paid microdata** funds manufacture of depth that the free tier does not give away as a host-level dump.

## Free aggregates

| Topic | Current lean | Still open |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| Who | Anyone, no account required | — |
| What | Headline tables, charts, methodology | Exact first table list; notebooks; rebuild SQL |
| License | CC BY 4.0 | Exact attribution sentence; Share-alike ever? |
| Deposit | Zenodo or Dataverse | Which venue; DOI per month vs per series |
| Embargo | None | Ever delay free release for a paid window? **Lean no.** |

Libraries may host mirrors of **free** aggregates. That should remain true after the first deposit.

## Academic and library access to microdata

Unwritten, to be answered in plain language before any outreach:

- Are aggregates always free regardless of affiliation? (**Lean yes.**)
- Microdata: education discount, free at thesis scale, or case-by-case?
- Classroom use of a paid extract?
- What a library can archive if they bought a snapshot?

Until those are written, do not promise “free microdata for all .edu.”

## Paid microdata (later)

Intended goods: Structure Index strata (Lite / Full), civic panel, script vectors, custom list enrichment.

Questions that must have answers before a first invoice:

- Internal analytics only, or may results appear in a client deliverable?
- May buyers **publish aggregates** derived from paid rows? (Probably yes, with rules.)
- Resale or inclusion in another commercial dataset? (Likely no.)
- Perpetual use of a snapshot vs subscription to the next crawl month?
- Identical rights across Lite / Full / Civic / Custom, or tiered?
- Delivery format (Parquet / CSV) and whether support is included.

There is no price list in this repository. There should not be one until a file exists to sell.

## Restricted cuts

- **Newsroom fingerprint / clustering** is ethics-gated. No access policy until an internal checklist exists.
- No PII brokerage. Domain + firmographic joins that start to look like a dossier need an explicit boundary.
- Law-enforcement or bulk surveillance requests: process not written. Default until then is **do not build for that buyer**.

## Font intellectual property (every pack)

Products describe **observed use and structure** on a public crawl.

They do **not** include font files, full glyph outlines for reuse, or a license to use any typeface.

Vendor IDs and family names are observational metadata, not an authorization chain.
